\begin{abstract}
This preprint introduces the Altitude Model, a geometric framework for 
interpretable artificial systems based on spectral alignment, operator 
governance, and conceptual quantum operator evolution. The model defines 
a ten-altitude progression (A0--A10) describing transitions from Euclidean 
geometry to spectral geometry, dual algebra, non-dual curvature, congruence 
geometry, complex bundles, quantum-inspired operators, and finally the 
Conceptual Quantum Operator Runtime (CQOR). Mathematical foundations are 
provided for proxy Hilbert geometry, density-operator semantics, conceptual 
CPTP-style evolution, spectral-governance constraints, and eco-governance 
bounds. Conceptual influences from CAUSA (Stell, 2026) are acknowledged 
for spectral-agency alignment and drift telemetry. EcoGrid (A3) provides 
the mathematical substrate for spectral governance and bounded operator 
evolution. This document outlines the theoretical foundations, mathematical 
structures, provenance architecture, limitations, and future work.
\end{abstract}
