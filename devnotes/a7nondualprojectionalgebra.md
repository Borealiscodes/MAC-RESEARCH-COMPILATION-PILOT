1. Dual Projection Operators (A6)

Dual projections satisfy:
\[
P^2 = P
\]

and preserve:
  • continuity  
  • adjacency  
  • spectral stability  
  • holonomy bounds  
  • determinism  
  • kernel structure  

Dual projections operate within the A6 manifold:
\[
P : M \to M.
\]

They maintain geometric correctness.

---

2. Non-Dual Projection Operators (A7)

Non-dual projection operators act on collapse surfaces:
\[
\Pi_{\mathrm{ND}} : M \to \Sigma.
\]

They satisfy:
\[
\Pi_{\mathrm{ND}}(p) = p \quad \text{if } p \in \Sigma.
\]

But they violate dual projection properties:
\[
\Pi{\mathrm{ND}}^2 \ne \Pi{\mathrm{ND}}.
\]

Non-dual projections are:
  • non-linear  
  • non-continuous  
  • non-adjacent  
  • spectrally discontinuous  
  • holonomy-breaking  
  • multi-valued  
  • non-idempotent  

They reconcile broken geometry.
