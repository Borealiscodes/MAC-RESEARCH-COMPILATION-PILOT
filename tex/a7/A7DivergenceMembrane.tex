\section*{Abstract}
This document defines the divergence membrane for A7 dual-algebra systems. The
divergence membrane is a geometric structure that captures the onset of
divergence behavior in operator execution. It generalizes the A6 safety envelope
and unified kernel by introducing divergence invariants, divergence operators,
divergence collapse surfaces, and divergence stability envelopes. The membrane
provides the boundary between stable A6 geometry and divergent A7 geometry.

\section{Divergence Geometry}
Let $X$ be the base manifold and $S$ the A6 safety envelope. The divergence
membrane $M \subset X$ is defined as:
\[
M = \{ p \in X \mid E(p) = \epsilon_S \},
\]
where $E$ is the stability-envelope function and $\epsilon_S$ is the global
safety threshold.

Thus, $M$ is the boundary of the A6 safety envelope:
\[
M = \partial S.
\]

\section{Divergence Invariants}
Divergence invariants measure deviation from A6 stability:
\[
I_{\text{div}}(p) = E(p) - \epsilon_S.
\]

A point is divergent when:
\[
I_{\text{div}}(p) > 0.
\]

Divergence invariants must satisfy:
\[
I_{\text{div}}(p) = 0 \quad \text{for all } p \in M.
\]

\section{Divergence Operators}
A divergence operator is a map:
\[
D_{\text{op}} : X \to X
\]
that increases divergence invariants:
\[
I_{\text{div}}(D_{\text{op}}(p)) \geq I_{\text{div}}(p).
\]

Operators must satisfy:
\[
p \in S \implies D_{\text{op}}(p) \in S \cup M.
\]

\section{Divergence Collapse Surfaces}
Divergence collapse surfaces $\Sigma_{\text{div}}$ are defined as:
\[
\Sigma_{\text{div}} = \{ p \mid I_{\text{div}}(p) = c \},
\]
for some constant $c > 0$.

These surfaces represent regions where divergence becomes geometrically
significant.

Collapse surfaces must satisfy:
\[
T_p M + T_p \Sigma_{\text{div}} = T_p X.
\]

\section{Divergence Stability Envelope}
The divergence stability envelope $E_{\text{div}}$ is defined as:
\[
E_{\text{div}}(p) = \max(0, I_{\text{div}}(p)).
\]

It measures the magnitude of divergence.

The envelope must satisfy:
\[
E_{\text{div}}(p) = 0 \quad \text{for } p \in M.
\]

\section{Divergence Kernel}
The divergence kernel $K_{\text{div}}$ is defined as:
\[
K_{\text{div}} = \{ p \mid I_{\text{div}}(p) = 0 \}.
\]

Thus:
\[
K_{\text{div}} = M.
\]

The divergence kernel acts as a firewall preventing uncontrolled divergence:
\[
p \in M \implies D_{\text{op}}(p) \in M.
\]

\section{Membrane Interaction}
The divergence membrane interacts with A6 geometry via:
\[
T_p M \subseteq T_p S.
\]

This ensures that divergence behavior is constrained by A6 stability.

\section*{Conclusion}
The divergence membrane defines the boundary between stable A6 geometry and
divergent A7 geometry. It introduces divergence invariants, divergence operators,
divergence collapse surfaces, divergence stability envelopes, and a divergence
kernel. This document provides the foundation for A7 operator algebra and
A7 runtime diagnostics.
