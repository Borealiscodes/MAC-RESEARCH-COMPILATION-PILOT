\section*{Abstract}
This document defines the operator algebra for A7 dual-algebra systems. A7
operators act on divergence geometry and divergence invariants, extending the
A6 operator stack into the divergent regime. These operators preserve the
divergence membrane, interact with divergence collapse surfaces, and propagate
divergence invariants in a controlled manner. This algebra forms the foundation
for A7 unified semantics and A7 runtime diagnostics.

\section{Divergence Operators}
Let $X$ be the base manifold and $M$ the divergence membrane. A divergence
operator is a map:
\[
D_{\text{op}} : X \to X
\]
that satisfies:
\[
I_{\text{div}}(D_{\text{op}}(p)) \geq I_{\text{div}}(p),
\]
where $I_{\text{div}}$ is the divergence invariant.

Thus, divergence operators are monotone with respect to divergence.

\section{Operator Classes}
A7 introduces five operator classes:
\begin{itemize}
  \item divergence spectral operators,
  \item divergence modal operators,
  \item divergence holonomy operators,
  \item divergence collapse operators,
  \item divergence stability operators.
\end{itemize}

Each class generalizes its A6 counterpart.

\section{Divergence Spectral Operators}
A divergence spectral operator $D_{\text{spec}}$ satisfies:
\[
| \delta(D_{\text{spec}}(p)) | \geq | \delta(p) |,
\]
where $\delta$ is the spectral mismatch.

These operators amplify spectral deviation.

\section{Divergence Modal Operators}
A divergence modal operator $D_{\text{modal}}$ satisfies:
\[
A(p,q) = \text{false} \implies A(D_{\text{modal}}(p), D_{\text{modal}}(q)) = \text{false}.
\]

These operators propagate modal discontinuities.

\section{Divergence Holonomy Operators}
A divergence holonomy operator $D_{\text{holo}}$ satisfies:
\[
|\tau(D_{\text{holo}}(p))| \geq |\tau(p)|,
\]
where $\tau$ is the holonomy drift.

These operators amplify curvature accumulation.

\section{Divergence Collapse Operators}
A divergence collapse operator $D_{\text{collapse}}$ satisfies:
\[
D_{\text{collapse}}(p) \in \Sigma_{\text{div}}
\quad \text{whenever} \quad p \in \Sigma_{\text{div}}.
\]

These operators preserve divergence collapse surfaces.

\section{Divergence Stability Operators}
A divergence stability operator $D_{\text{stab}}$ satisfies:
\[
E_{\text{div}}(D_{\text{stab}}(p)) \geq E_{\text{div}}(p),
\]
where $E_{\text{div}}$ is the divergence stability envelope.

These operators amplify divergence magnitude.

\section{Operator Composition}
Operator composition must satisfy:
\[
I_{\text{div}}(D_2(D_1(p))) \geq I_{\text{div}}(p).
\]

Thus, the operator algebra is closed under composition.

\section{Membrane Preservation}
All divergence operators must preserve the divergence membrane:
\[
p \in M \implies D_{\text{op}}(p) \in M.
\]

This ensures that divergence behavior remains constrained.

\section{Collapse-Surface Interaction}
Divergence operators must preserve transversal intersection:
\[
T_p M + T_p \Sigma_{\text{div}} = T_p X.
\]

This ensures that divergence collapse surfaces remain geometrically stable.

\section{Stability Interaction}
Divergence operators must satisfy:
\[
E_{\text{div}}(D_{\text{op}}(p)) \geq E_{\text{div}}(p).
\]

This ensures that divergence magnitude does not decrease under operator action.

The A7 operator algebra defines the operator structure acting on divergence
