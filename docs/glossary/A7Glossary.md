1. Divergence Invariant
The divergence invariant measures deviation from the A6 safety threshold:

\[
I{\text{div}}(p) = E(p) - \epsilonS
\]

It is the fundamental scalar quantity of A7 geometry.

---

2. Divergence Membrane
The divergence membrane M is the boundary of the A6 safety envelope:

\[
M = \partial S = \{ p \mid E(p) = \epsilon_S \}
\]

It is the geometric surface where non‑dual algebra becomes admissible.

3. Divergence Kernel
The divergence kernel is the set of points with zero divergence:

\[
K{\text{div}} = \{ p \mid I{\text{div}}(p) = 0 \}
\]

In A7, the divergence kernel coincides with the membrane itself.

---

4. Divergence Stability Envelope
The divergence stability envelope measures the magnitude of divergence:

\[
E{\text{div}}(p) = \max(0, I{\text{div}}(p))
\]

It generalizes the A6 stability envelope into the divergent regime.

---

5. Divergence Collapse Surface
A divergence collapse surface Σ_div is a level set of the divergence invariant:

\[
\Sigma{\text{div}} = \{ p \mid I{\text{div}}(p) = c \}, \quad c > 0
\]

These surfaces represent regions where divergence becomes geometrically
significant.

6. Divergence Spectral Tearing
Spectral tearing is the A7 analogue of spectral continuity. It describes
discontinuous jumps in the spectral mismatch function δ(p).

Key property:  
\[
|\delta(D_{\text{spec}}(p))| \ge |\delta(p)|
\]

7. Divergence Modal Fracture
Modal fracture is the A7 analogue of modal adjacency. It describes the breaking

Key property:  
If A(p,q) is false, then A(D(p), D(q)) remains false.

8. Divergence Holonomy Accumulation

Key property:  
\[

\]

9. Divergence Operators

\[

\[

They are the core operators of A7 non‑dual algebra.

---

10. Membrane Preservation
All divergence operators must preserve the divergence membrane:

\[
p \in M \implies D_{\text{op}}(p) \in M
\]

This ensures that non‑dual algebra remains constrained to the membrane.

---

11. Transversal Interaction
Divergence collapse surfaces must intersect the membrane transversally:

\[
Tp M + Tp \Sigma{\text{div}} = Tp X
\]

This ensures geometric stability of divergence behavior.
