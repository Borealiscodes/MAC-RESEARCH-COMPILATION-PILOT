\begin{abstract}
Industry AI safety relies heavily on heuristic interpretability tools such as
saliency maps, sparse autoencoders, behavioral probes, and RLHF reward shaping.
These methods provide diagnostic insight but lack structural invariants. In
contrast, the altitude model (A3--A6) defines geometric and algebraic runtime
physics that enforce curvature continuity, adjacency preservation, spectral
stability, modal lift, provenance lift, and kernel invariants. This preprint
presents a formal comparison between heuristic interpretability and structural
interpretability, culminating in the dual-algebra runtime layer at A6 and the
A6.5 firewall. We argue that structural interpretability provides mathematically
grounded guarantees absent from heuristic approaches, and we outline the
implications for future AI systems as they approach A7 non-dual algebra.
\end{abstract}
