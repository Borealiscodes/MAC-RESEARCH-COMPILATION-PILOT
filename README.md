# 🌲 README.md — MAC‑RESEARCH‑COMPILATION‑PILOT
Multi‑Altitude Citation Ontologies • Structural AI • Provenance Guarding • Formal Verification • Research Ecosystem Compilation

Why This System Matters

Modern research ecosystems are fragmented, inconsistent, and difficult to navigate — especially for independent researchers, human‑rights defenders, and people working under constrained conditions. MAC‑RESEARCH‑COMPILATION‑PILOT exists to solve a structural problem:

> Research needs altitude, membrane, provenance, and accessibility — not just content.

This repository demonstrates how a research ecosystem can be:

- structured  
- altitude‑aware  
- provenance‑safe  
- formally verifiable  
- operator‑bounded  
- accessible to non‑institutional researchers  

As accessibility layers in Triangle Mutiny mature, this system becomes even more approachable:

- NDH case studies become easier to write  
- MAC ontologies become easier to navigate  
- provenance becomes easier to understand  
- structural AI concepts become teachable  
- independent researchers gain tools normally reserved for institutions  

This project is not just a scaffold — it is a pathway.
